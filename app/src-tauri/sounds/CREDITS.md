# Sound credits

Clink.wav, Ding.wav and Done.wav are trimmed, downmixed to mono and resampled to 22.05 kHz from these Freesound recordings (Clink and Ding peak-normalized, Done loudness-matched to Ding), all released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required, but given anyway.

| File | Source | Author |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clink.wav | [clinking glass.aiff](https://freesound.org/s/104815/) | TheWah |
| Ding.wav | [Soft-Notifications - Bell - Ding-Dong.mp3](https://freesound.org/s/571513/) | LegitCheese |
| Done.wav | [done sound](https://freesound.org/s/808521/) | Licht2003 |

The other sounds here are synthesized by `scripts/gen-sounds.py`.
